# 🗺️ Busundo Feature Access Guide

## How to Access All Viral Features

---

## 📍 **Navigation Menu**

All viral features are now accessible through the **"Features"** dropdown menu in the navigation bar!

### **Location:** Top navigation bar → **Features** (with dropdown arrow)

---

## 🎯 **Available Features**

### 1. **My Busundo** 🔥
**Path:** `/my-busundo`  
**Access:** Features dropdown → My Busundo

**What's Inside:**
- ⭐ **Commute Score** - Your overall travel performance (0-100)
- 🌱 **Eco Score** - Environmental impact rating
- 🔥 **Daily Streak** - Consecutive days of activity
- 💰 **Fuel Saved** - Money saved vs solo travel
- 🌍 **CO₂ Saved** - Carbon offset in kg
- 🚗 **Shared Rides** - Total carpools joined
- ⚡ **Travel Karma** - Reputation points
- 🏅 **Badges** - Achievement collection
- 🎁 **Travel Wrapped** - Year-end summary (shareable!)

**Why Use It:**
- Track your travel impact
- Build your reputation
- Share your achievements on Instagram
- Maintain your streak for bonus karma

---

### 2. **Crowd Meter** 🚍
**Path:** `/crowd-meter`  
**Access:** Features dropdown → Crowd Meter

**What's Inside:**
- 😊 **Report Crowd Levels** - One-tap emoji reporting
- 📊 **Live Data** - Real-time bus crowd levels (0-100%)
- 🎯 **Smart Recommendations** - "Board", "Wait", or "Avoid"
- ⏱️ **Next Bus Timer** - Minutes until next bus
- 📈 **Progress Bars** - Visual crowd indicators

**Why Use It:**
- Avoid packed buses
- Know before you go
- Help others with your reports
- Earn 10 karma per report

**Unique Feature:** Google Maps doesn't have this!

---

### 3. **Lost & Found** 🎒
**Path:** `/lost-found`  
**Access:** Features dropdown → Lost & Found

**What's Inside:**
- 📦 **Report Lost Items** - Post what you lost
- ✅ **Report Found Items** - Help reunite items
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** - By route, category, date
- 💬 **Contact System** - Connect with finders/losers
- 📊 **Success Stats** - 76% reunion rate!

**Categories:**
- Bags & Backpacks
- Electronics (phones, laptops)
- Documents (ID cards, certificates)
- Personal Items (water bottles, umbrellas)
- Clothing
- Other

**Why Use It:**
- Lost something? Community helps!
- Found something? Be a hero!
- Earn 20 karma for posting
- Earn 30 bonus karma for resolving

**Killer Feature:** People install Busundo just for this!

---

### 4. **Bus Buddy Match** 👥
**Path:** `/buddy-match`  
**Access:** Features dropdown → Bus Buddy Match

**What's Inside:**
- 👤 **Create Profile** - Share your commute schedule
- 🎯 **Smart Matching** - 95% compatibility algorithm
- 🗺️ **Common Routes** - Find buddies on your route
- ⏰ **Schedule Overlap** - Match commute times
- 🎨 **Interests** - Connect over shared hobbies
- 💬 **Connect** - Message and coordinate

**Match Algorithm:**
- 50% - Common routes
- 30% - Schedule overlap
- 20% - Shared interests

**Why Use It:**
- Make friends on your commute
- Travel together safely
- Share stories and experiences
- Earn 25 karma for connecting

**Not Dating:** This is for travel companions only!
